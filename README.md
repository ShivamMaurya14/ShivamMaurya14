### 👋 About Me

I am a **Robotics Software & Applied AI Engineer** based in Pune, India, currently pursuing **B.E. in Automation & Robotics Engineering** at the **Army Institute of Technology (AIT), Pune (2024–2028)**.

- 🦾 **Manipulation & Digital Twin:** Built a 3× UR10e + Robotiq 2F-140 workcell running on **ROS 2 Jazzy**, **MoveIt 2**, **Isaac Sim**, and **Gazebo Harmonic**, featuring isolated namespaces (`/robot1..3`), custom `PickPlace.action`, and an autonomous workpiece relay (A→B→C→D).
- ⚡ **Low-Level Controls & Bridges:** Authored `ros2_control` `SystemInterface` plugins with binary CRC8 UART (>1 kHz target) and Micro-ROS modes on STM32 for real-time motor actuation.
- 🚗 **Mobile Autonomy:** Designed modular Nav2 architectures with explicit lifecycle control (AMCL, DWB, SLAM Toolbox) and custom recovery Behavior Trees for GPS-denied environments.
- 👁️ **High-Throughput Perception:** Developed competition-grade vision nodes executing at **257 FPS (3.89 ms)** ONNX on CPU for ABU Robocon 2026.
- 🧠 **Agentic AI & Healthcare ML:** Built LangGraph-driven multi-agent underwriting engines (Hackathon 2026 Runner-up), Indic voice clinical triage with Sarvam + Gemini, and CLSI-aligned AMR predictors (IIT BHU).
- 🌐 **Open-Source Contributor:** 15+ merged PRs across core ROS 2 repositories (`navigation2`, `ros2_control`, `ros2_controllers`, `moveit2`).
- 🎓 **Leadership:** Software Lead at the **Centre of Excellence for AI & Robotics (CEAR), AIT Pune**, leading 100+ student researchers across competitive robotics and applied engineering.

| 🏆 [**Robocon-Team-CEAR**](https://github.com/ShivamMaurya14/Robocon-Team-CEAR) | CAD (Fusion 360), SolidWorks, Kinematics, ROS 2 | • Official Stage-I design and engineering packet for Team CEAR at **ABU Robocon 2026 (All India Rank < 20)**.<br>• Complete CAD models for R1 & R2 competition robots, planetary drive base, and custom vertical lifting mechanisms.<br>• Rigorous actuator torque, center-of-mass, and dynamic acceleration calculations + strategic arena playbook. |
